How we work

A clear, step-by-step approach to every job

At Sunshine Ducts, we believe professional cleaning should be simple and stress-free. Our team follows a clear, step-by-step process — from your first call and on-site inspection to the cleaning itself and a final walkthrough. Every stage is handled with the same care, whether it's a single vent or a full system inspection.

01. Initial Inspection & Scope Confirmation

Every service begins with an on-site inspection of accessible ductwork, vents, and equipment. We identify visible buildup or concerns and confirm the exact scope of work with you before anything starts — no guesswork, no surprise charges.

02. Professional Cleaning & Service

Once the scope is agreed, our technicians get to work using professional-grade equipment. We protect your floors and work areas as we clean the agreed ductwork, vents, and components — with attention to detail at every step.

03. Final Walkthrough & Recommendations

When the work is done, we walk you through what was cleaned, share any observations we made, and offer honest recommendations for future maintenance. You'll leave the conversation knowing exactly what was done and what to watch for next.

Why it matters

How clean ducts support a more efficient home

When dust, lint, and debris build up inside your duct system, your HVAC equipment has to work harder to move air. Regular professional cleaning helps support better airflow and lets your heating and cooling system run the way it was designed to.

01

Better airflow throughout your home

Removing built-up dust and debris from accessible ductwork helps air move more freely through your system.

02

Less strain on your HVAC system

When airflow is restricted, your system has to work harder. Cleaner ducts help reduce that unnecessary strain.

03

A healthier indoor environment

Reducing accumulated dust in your ductwork is one step toward cleaner air circulating through your home.

Most homes benefit from a professional duct cleaning every 2–3 years, though this can vary based on your home's age, whether you have pets, recent renovations, and how often your HVAC system runs. Homes with heavy dust buildup, allergy concerns, or recent construction may need more frequent cleaning.

A full system inspection covers accessible ductwork, supply and return vents, and the relevant heating and cooling components we can safely reach. We document visible buildup or concerns, confirm the scope of any recommended cleaning, and provide a clear summary of our findings along with honest recommendations for next steps.

Most residential jobs take between 2 and 4 hours depending on the size of the home, the number of vents, and the amount of buildup. Dryer vent cleaning is typically much quicker, usually 30–60 minutes. Your technician will give you a time estimate before starting.

We recommend being home for the initial inspection and the final walkthrough, so you can ask questions and see exactly what we've done. You don't need to be present the entire time — many customers step out during the cleaning itself. Just let us know your preference when scheduling.
