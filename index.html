What we do

A clear, step-by-step process from start to finish

At Sunshine Ducts, we make professional cleaning simple and stress-free. Every job follows a clear process — from your first call and on-site inspection to the cleaning itself and a final walkthrough. You'll know exactly what we're doing at each stage, so there are no surprises.

01. Initial Inspection & Scope Confirmation

Every service begins with an on-site inspection of accessible ductwork, vents, and equipment. We identify visible buildup or concerns and confirm the exact scope of work with you before anything starts — no guesswork, no surprise charges.

02. Professional Cleaning & Service

Once the scope is agreed, our technicians get to work using professional-grade equipment. We protect your floors and work areas as we clean the agreed ductwork, vents, and components — with attention to detail at every step.

03. Final Walkthrough & Recommendations

When the work is done, we walk you through what was cleaned, share any observations we made, and offer honest recommendations for future maintenance. You'll leave the conversation knowing exactly what was done and what to watch for next.

Why it matters

How clean ducts support a more efficient home

When dust, lint, and debris build up inside your duct system, your HVAC equipment has to work harder to move air. Regular professional cleaning helps support better airflow and lets your heating and cooling system run the way it was designed to.

01

Better airflow throughout your home

Removing built-up dust and debris from accessible ductwork helps air move more freely through your system.

02

Less strain on your HVAC system

When airflow is restricted, your system has to work harder. Cleaner ducts help reduce that unnecessary strain.

03

A healthier indoor environment

Reducing accumulated dust in your ductwork is one step toward cleaner air circulating through your home.
